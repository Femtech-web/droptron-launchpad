# Droptron

**Private participation and private token distribution for Starknet launches.**

Droptron is a Starknet token-launch and distribution platform built for the STRK20 Private Sprint. Launch price and market action remain public; STRK20 protects participant identity, private token allocations, distributions, and shielded balances.

## Product scope

1. Private launch participation followed by a shielded token allocation.
2. Private team and treasury distributions.
3. Private airdrop claiming through private entitlement notes.
4. Private multi-tranche vesting claims delivered as shielded balances.

## Privacy promise

Droptron does not claim a fully confidential bonding curve. Shield and unshield addresses, amounts, and timing are public. The launch price and public contract activity are also public. Private STRK20 transfers protect the transfer parties, amount, token, and private balances. A private claim proves possession of a previously delivered private entitlement note; it does not make public market activity invisible.

## Status

Planning and repository setup; no contract is deployed and no mainnet transaction has been made from this repository.

## Hackathon requirements

Before the deadline this repository needs a public demo, a three-minute demo video, and three successful Starknet mainnet transaction hashes touching the STRK20 pool. See [the hackathon](https://strk20.starknet.io/hackathon) and [STRK20 by Example](https://strk20-by-example.org/what-is-strk20).
